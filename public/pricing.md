# Calisto Pricing

All plans are priced **per event** (one-time payment, not a subscription).
Each plan sets limits for that specific event only.

---

## Free — 0€ / event

Tailored for: small birthdays and family gatherings

- Photos: 20
- Videos: 0
- Guest limit: 5
- Guests can upload for: 3 days after the event date
- Photos kept for: 7 days after the event date
- Download all: full gallery ZIP, 24-hour link (primary organizer)

---

## Notes

- Fair-use policy applies: a reasonable maximum file size per video is enforced to prevent abuse.
- Uploads exceeding the per-file size limit are rejected with a clear error message.
- Organizers can delete individual photos or videos from the gallery at any time.
- The full album can be deleted early via Settings before the automatic retention cutoff.
