# Calisto glossary

- **Invitation draft**: The editable invitation artwork and event wording owned by the primary organizer.
- **Published invitation**: A frozen copy of an invitation draft that guests can view and answer against.
- **Household**: One recipient group that receives a personal digital invitation link and submits one RSVP.
- **Attendee**: A named person the household confirms as attending. Attendees do not need Calisto accounts.
- **Event membership**: A Calisto account's access to an event and its gallery. It is separate from a household or attendee.
